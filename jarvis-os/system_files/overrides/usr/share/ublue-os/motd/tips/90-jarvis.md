JARVIS Assistant is built in

Ask it anything about this machine — it can read your GPU, services and logs
without sending a byte to the internet. It can also act: apps, volume, services,
updates and more, with confirmation for risky commands.

  Open it:      JARVIS Assistant in the app grid, or `ujust jarvis-ai`
  Talk to it:   say "Jarvis, volume up" or press Super+Alt+J
  Voice setup:  `ujust jarvis-voice-setup`
  Add models:   `ujust jarvis-ai-models`
  Re-run setup: `ujust jarvis-setup`
